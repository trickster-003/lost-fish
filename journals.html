𓆜⋆ 29 aug - J. R. CARPENTER / A Handmade Web

I found this reading to be very interesting and a little sad at what the world of web has to offer today. and it reminded me of one of my fav artists' websites as well, Dave Mckean. For this entry I want to paste a few parts from the text that I found most interesting!
“The term 'handmade' usually refers to objects made by hand or by using simple tools rather than machines. The result may be homely — as in a child's clay ashtray — or exquisite — as in a pair of bespoke brogues.” I really liked the comparisons of how a website is made by hand not machine like a kid’s clay ashtray— and it reminded me of one of my fav artists websites Dave Mckean.
“...When people got to the end they stopped reading because that’s the way books work. In the web version, the last page linked to the first; the story circled round and round.” This made me think about how much of a loop websites are and how endless they can become
“Whereas archives held in museums or libraries generally contain artifacts created elsewhere — manuscripts illuminated in a monastery, for example, or photographs developed in a darkroom — the handmade web pages contained in this online archive continue to exist in the medium within which they were created. That said, the frames through which we view them continue to change.” I like how with time the view on websites especially changes, the coming back of analog websites how just a few years ago they were viewed very differently. Also reminded me a bit about older pixilated games and how they were mainly made for CRT TVs and the pixels weren’t as visible and blended the pixels but now they are and became its own style of pixel games.
“The hand is made to work. Through the labour of the body the virtual space of the browser window is transformed into an actual, physical distance.” made me think a lot about the replacement of humans to do a lot of these jobs getting laid off and such and how sites feel more meaningful when made by hand
